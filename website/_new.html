Where we disagree

Four opinions most
email AI doesn't hold.

These are choices, not features. Each one costs Klorn something, and each one is the reason to use it instead of the assistant already bundled with your mail.

01 · Autonomy

An agent that acts for you is the wrong product.

Everyone is shipping autonomy. The scarce thing is judgement, not labour — you can write the reply in ninety seconds, but deciding which of forty messages deserves it is what eats the morning. Klorn does the deciding and leaves the acting to you.

Send, delete and forward all wait for you · 0 unapproved actions
02 · Model

Four tiers. Not folders, labels or scores.

A confidence percentage tells you nothing you can act on, and unlimited labels become a second inbox. A message either interrupts you, waits, is filed, or was handled. There is no fifth tier.

Every message shows why it landed there
03 · Evidence

A number you can check beats a promise.

"AI-powered" is not a claim. Klorn is scored against hand-labeled real mail before every release, cold and warm reported separately, and the set is in the repo so you can disagree with our labels.

94.3% warm · 81.1% cold · the set →
04 · Exit

You should be able to leave, and to look inside first.

An email agent reads everything you receive. That is too much power to hand to a box you can neither inspect nor exit. Klorn is AGPLv3, self-hosts against your own database and model keys, and caps its own daily spend so a runaway loop can't bill you.

Built, not planned

Four things that exist
nowhere else.

Not a spec sheet — these are the four reasons to move, and all four ship in the current macOS build.

A bar, not another inbox only here

Klorn lives in one native line at the top of the screen. It never steals focus while you work, and there is no fifth window to check.

The reason, on every message only here

"You replied to this sender 6 times." You can see why it was ranked, disagree in one click, and the classifier learns from the correction.

Set the tone once only here

Formal, friendly, casual — or learned from your own sent mail. It shapes all three reply drafts without changing what they say, in whatever language the sender wrote in.

A receipt for every action only here

Send, delete and forward each mint a signed receipt with a payload hash, so what you approved is provably what went out.
